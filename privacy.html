Privacy Policy

Five Out, by Naik Studios. Effective 31 October 2026.

Five Out is a finger-counting game you play against computer players on your own phone. We built it so that it does not collect any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. Five Out does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

The app saves a few settings on your device so it remembers them next time:

  • the style you picked (for example Notebook or Neon night)
  • your last match setup (number of players, mode, difficulty)
  • your hint and vibration settings, and which modes you have already played

This information never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Internet and permissions

Five Out works fully offline. The only device feature it uses is vibration, for feedback during play. It does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Children

Five Out does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that handle data, such as online play with friends, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date at the top.
