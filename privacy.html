Privacy Policy

Five Out, by Sagar Naik (Naik Studios). Effective 31 October 2026.

Five Out is a finger-counting game. You can play against computer players on your phone, or with friends online. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Playing on your own

Matches against computer players run entirely on your phone. The app saves your style, match setup, hint and vibration settings, and the name you use for online play on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Playing with friends online

When you use Play with friends, the app creates an anonymous player ID for you. We do not ask for your email, phone number or real name. We store:

  • the display name you type in, which other players in your room can see
  • your anonymous player ID
  • the rooms you create or join, and the moves made in each match
  • how many online matches you have started each day, to apply your plan's daily limit

This is stored with our database provider, Supabase. Rooms are closed after a day without activity. You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see Contact).

Ads

On the Free plan, the app shows ads from Google AdMob between matches, and an optional ad you can watch for an extra online game. We ask Google for non-personalised ads only. Google may still collect device information, such as an advertising ID, IP address and basic device details, to show ads, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Basic and Premium plans have no ads.

Purchases

Plans are bought through Google Play or the App Store. We never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check which plan you have. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history from the store.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • The only device feature the game uses is vibration, for feedback during play.

Children

Five Out is meant for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has used online play, contact us and we will delete their data.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects, we will update this page and the date at the top before releasing that version.

Contact

Questions, or a request to delete your data: naik.saagar10@gmail.com. Please include your display name and roughly when you played online.
