Delete your data

Five Out: The Classic Game, by Sagar Naik (Naik Studios).

Five Out has no accounts. If you have played online with friends, you can ask us to delete the information stored for online play.

How to request deletion

  1. Email naik.saagar10@gmail.com with the subject Five Out data deletion.
  2. Include the display name you used in online rooms and roughly when you last played online, so we can find your data.
  3. We will delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What we delete

  • Your anonymous player ID
  • The display name you entered for online rooms
  • Rooms you created or joined, and the moves in those matches
  • Your record of how many online matches you started each day

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchases: subscription records are kept by Google Play or the App Store and by RevenueCat, as required for billing and tax. Manage or cancel plans in your Google Play or App Store subscriptions.
  • Ads: Google AdMob handles its own ad data under Google's policies. We ask for non-personalised ads only.
  • On your phone: your settings and style are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Online rooms are also closed automatically after a day without activity.
