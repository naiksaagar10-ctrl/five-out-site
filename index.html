Five Out

The finger game from the last bench. Everyone starts with one finger up on each hand. Hit a hand to add your count. Exactly five knocks it out.

Modes

  • Classic: the game as played in school, no powers.
  • Lucky Draw: draw power cards from the deck.
  • Pick Your Power: choose one secret power before the match.
  • Wild Power: give up a turn for a random power.

Play against the computer, or with friends online using a 4-letter room code. Play 1v1, or 3 and 4 player matches at a card table, in four styles: Neon night, Notebook, Cartoon arcade and Mumbai street pop.
